# AI Reading Club (Kickoff Announcement)

## Headline

Join the AI Reading Club: From BPE to Transformers to Alignment (14-paper curriculum)

## Body

I am starting an AI Reading Club to read and discuss the papers that shaped modern LLMs. The goal is to understand the engineering ideas behind the systems we use every day: tokenisation, self-attention, decoding, data foundations, efficiency, fine-tuning, and alignment.

The club starts on **Wednesday, 2026-03-11**. From **Thursday, 2026-05-14** onwards, sessions continue every two weeks on Thursdays.

### How it works

- Cadence: one paper every two weeks
- Format: 10-15 minute volunteer overview, followed by about 45 minutes of discussion
- Summer break: No sessions in August
- Holidays: If a session lands on a holiday, we skip that week and meet on the next scheduled session date
